REFERENCE ID: NBD-FIELD-209
TOPIC: Survey control, QA/QC and positional accuracy

DEFINE QUALITY
Specify reference CRS, horizontal datum, vertical datum, epoch if relevant, units, accuracy class, acceptable tolerances, coverage completeness and verification procedure. Accuracy is not the same as pixel resolution, point density or model detail.

CONTROL PLANNING
Agree control survey source and method, GCPs as applicable, independent checkpoints, accessibility, distribution over the study area and whether transformation/calibration is needed. Independent validation points should not all be used to fit the adjustment they evaluate.

QA RECORDS
Checklists for data gaps, geometry/registration errors, class-label consistency, topology, coordinate offsets, deliverable formats, file integrity, metadata completeness and revision tracking. Record failures and rework before final acceptance.

CLIENT QUESTIONS
Who supplies survey control? Which accuracy reporting convention is required? Is a test sample required? Who signs off deliverables? What file naming, tiling, versioning and coordinate grids apply?
