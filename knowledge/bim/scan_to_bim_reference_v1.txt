REFERENCE ID: NBD-BIM-205
TOPIC: Laser scanning and Scan-to-BIM requirement intake

SCOPE DISTINCTION
Laser scanning creates measured point clouds. Scan-to-BIM transforms appropriate scan evidence into modeled building elements. Point cloud delivery and authored BIM are distinct services.

NECESSARY DETAILS
Building type, floor area, floors and accessible spaces; source scans or need for new scanning; survey control and coordinate alignment; required disciplines (architecture, structure, MEP etc.); level of information need / expected geometric detail; permitted tolerances; model versions; heritage/deformation concerns; restrictions and target completion date.

POTENTIAL DELIVERABLES
Registered E57/LAS/RCP point cloud as agreed; BIM in RVT and/or IFC; floor plans, elevations, sections, clash or QA reports; element schedules. Never assume MEP because an architectural model was requested.

QUALITY VALIDATION
Registration residuals, cloud-to-model checks, modeled versus unmodeled elements, independent spot checks, control alignment, export validation and issue logs. Avoid treating visual similarity as measured accuracy.

COMMON AMBIGUITIES
'LOD 300' or similar alone may be insufficient; ask the client to specify discipline, element lists, tolerance and information need. 'As built' may require site access and verification rather than assumptions from old drawings.

CASE
'8-floor office, 18,000 sqm, Revit model' - ask whether source point cloud exists, scanning scope, disciplines, versions, geometric tolerance and floor access; do not invent MEP requirements.
