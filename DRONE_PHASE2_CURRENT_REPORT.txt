CURRENT RELEASE: Drone Operational Phase 2

The Drone module now supports real connected operations rather than register-only viewing.

A dispatch or assignment creates:
1. operation header and item records
2. current asset/kit project, custodian, location and status updates
3. movement records
4. automatic Drone work record
5. project-detail timeline updates
6. dashboard active-operation and recent-movement updates

Return supports full or partial quantities. Transfer preserves previous custody in movement history. Quantity-managed assets show total, allocated and available quantities.

IT remains separate:
- IT Work Records stays at /work and is not shown to Drone role
- Drone Work Records is at /drone/work-records
- Drone Operations is at /drone/operations
- IT role cannot access Drone operational APIs

Next planned phase:
- maintenance
- calibration
- physical verification and missing items
- UIN compliance workflow
- HDD delivery chain of custody
- Drone monthly/yearly reports and frozen snapshots
