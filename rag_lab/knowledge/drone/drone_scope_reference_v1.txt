REFERENCE ID: NBD-DRONE-201
TOPIC: Drone/UAV mapping requirement intake

PURPOSE
Understand whether the client seeks imagery, terrain models, inspection, volumetrics or another use case. A drone flight is a data collection method and not itself a deliverable.

FIRST QUESTIONS
1. Exact site boundary and approximate area; 2. Site location, access and airspace constraints; 3. Intended application and season/weather limitations; 4. Required GSD; 5. Horizontal/vertical accuracy and coordinate datum; 6. Available GCP/checkpoint network; 7. Confirmed products and output format; 8. Deadline and acceptance criteria.

COMMON POTENTIAL PRODUCTS
Georeferenced orthophoto mosaic, dense photogrammetric point cloud, DSM, bare-earth DTM if feasible with adequate ground information, contours, CAD linework, 3D mesh, stockpile volume report. Not every product is feasible or included; confirm feasibility and commercial scope.

CRITICAL TECHNICAL DISTINCTIONS
GSD describes ground distance represented by an image pixel; it is NOT the same as guaranteed positional accuracy. Ground control and independent checkpoints must be planned according to agreed accuracy requirements. Dense vegetation can limit photogrammetric bare-earth visibility. Solar farms, construction sites and urban building sites require different workflows and verification.

EXAMPLE A
Client: 'Survey 350 acres; deliver an orthophoto and DSM within three weeks.' Confirmed: 350 acres, orthophoto, DSM, three-week target. Missing: location, GSD, accuracy, boundary, CRS and operational constraints. Do not add DTM as a confirmed output.

EXAMPLE B
Client: 'Map 100 acres; 5 cm GSD, GeoTIFF orthophoto only.' Confirmed: 100 acres, 5 cm GSD, GeoTIFF orthophoto only. Do not insert DSM or DTM into confirmed outputs.
