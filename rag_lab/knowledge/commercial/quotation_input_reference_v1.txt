REFERENCE ID: NBD-COMMERCIAL-210
TOPIC: Gathering inputs for a geospatial project quotation

QUOTE INPUTS
Service category, measurable extent with units, source-data availability, field collection method, site accessibility and mobilization restrictions, technical accuracy, deliverables and format, number of revisions, schedule, QA requirements, field/office split, assumptions, exclusions, permitted vendors and review/approval owners.

NO AUTOMATIC PRICING
Never produce a fixed price, unit rate, personnel cost, profit margin or invoice figure from a technical knowledge document unless an authorized, current commercial source explicitly provides it and policy permits release. Internal Finance data must not be exposed to ordinary BD staff via RAG.

SCOPE-CHANGE RULE
A change from orthophoto-only to orthophoto+DTM is a potential scope revision, not an assumption. A 20 km corridor without width cannot be turned into an area for pricing. Do not confuse estimated effort with confirmed project budget.

EXAMPLE
Client: 'Need classification of 12 million supplied LiDAR points, five classes, within ten days.' Known: existing data, quantity, broad classes count, proposed deadline. Missing: source file sample/quality, precise class definitions, classification acceptance rules, CRS and deliverable schema. Do not invent an INR quote.

SAFETY
Price approvals and any approval chain remain governed by existing ERP workflows, not by model recommendations.
