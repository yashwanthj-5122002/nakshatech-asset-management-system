# Drone Survey - RAG Training Example

Reference code: DEMO-DRONE-042

Service: Drone Survey

For this training example, collect the following
information before preparing a survey scope:

1. Site location
2. Survey area
3. Required deliverables
4. Required GSD or positional accuracy
5. Expected completion deadline

Possible deliverables:
- Orthophoto
- DSM
- DTM
- Contour drawings

Rules:
- Never invent project quantities or accuracy.
- If GSD is missing, ask the client.
- Do not assume all deliverables are required.
- Keep technical recommendations separate from
  the client's confirmed requirements.
