REFERENCE ID: NBD-SURVEY-208
TOPIC: Topographic and cadastral survey requirement intake

DISTINGUISH SERVICES
Topographic mapping describes measured terrain/features; cadastral survey addresses parcel boundaries and must follow the relevant land-record authority's legal procedures. A GIS plot overlay is not proof of legal title or an authorized boundary determination.

REQUIRED INFORMATION
District/taluk/village or relevant locality, authoritative survey/parcel identifiers, land parcel extent, boundary purpose, available records, scale, target precision, control reference, access and permission status, dispute status, expected outputs and approving agency.

WORKFLOW EXAMPLE
For multi-scale cadastral mapping, feature matching may proceed from plot survey to village map, administrative subdivisions and district/state layers. This is a proposed data conflation process and requires authoritative source lineage, coordinate transformation checks, topology testing and field/record validation.

OUTPUT OPTIONS
Survey drawings, georeferenced parcel polygon layers, cadastral map PDF/image, coordinate schedules, metadata and discrepancy reports. Data extraction and redisplay from government portals require permitted APIs, licensing and suitable disclaimers.

DO NOT CLAIM
No legal ownership conclusions, official certification, exact boundary validity or map reproduction rights without authoritative evidence and permission.
